Building Automation

Programmed controllers (DDC+PLC)

DigiPoint digital programmable controller

DigiPoint is an independent controller that includes an internal clock that can be programmed using a ladder diagram and software routines developed for different uses, the controller was specially designed for controlling and monitoring lighting systems and electromechanical systems in public and industrial buildings.

The controller is specially adapted for hotels, office buildings, hospitals, universities, museums, clean industry and more.

Superbrain FC Visit the Nachshon blower units

SuperBrain FC is a programmable controller that has been designed Especially for controlling air conditioners, blower units, AW units VAV units and more.

SuperBrain FC is the optimal solution for unit control Air conditioning with several fan speed settings, heater control Control of hot / cold water taps and motorized blinds.

VeroPoint controller with expansion cards

VeroPoint is a standalone and universal programmable controller (PLC/DDC) specially designed for monitoring and control purposes of heating, ventilation, and cooling systems (HVAC), control systems Electricity and energy and closed loop control.

VeroPoint is the optimal solution for system control and monitoring In public buildings, industry, hospitals, hotels.

SuperBrain controller with software library

SuperBrain is a specially designed standalone controller (DDC).

For control and measurement of air conditioning, ventilation and heating systems (HVAC) including energy systems, lighting and other electro-mechanical systems in the building.

Parking lot control

CO gas monitoring and control systems

UniCo is a sensor designed for measuring the level of toxic gases of the oxygen type Monocarbon CO which is a byproduct of the operation of gasoline engines in parking lots.

The feeling has excellent stability, including a low level of reaction to changes in temperature and other gases found in the parking lot.

Smart parking

The IGP smart parking system makes use of advanced computer vision technology in order to identify vacant parking or occupied parking and then correspondingly turns on an appropriate green/red/blue signal of vacant or occupied parking.

The system includes the option of up to 2 cameras which enable the identification of up to 3 parking lots in one unit.

Plumbing control

Elnet xp controller for drainage and sewage pumps

An automatic switching controller is used to operate drainage and sewage pumps and is found in almost every public building today, the controller is used to automatically control the level of rainwater or sewage, including automatic switching between two pumps in each operation in order to balance the working hours and the introduction of an off-duty pump for reinforcement when necessary.

We find these systems in factories, hotels, hospitals, universities, office buildings and any other public building where a reliable system is required to operate pumps for draining rainwater or sewage.

Flood detection systems

Flooding sensor

The FLs Flooding Sensor is an electronic sensor designed for the detection of any type of flooding or liquid spillage and the prevention of any possible damage caused from such flooding.

The sensor is specially designed for applications of flood detection in commercial buildings and planned specifically for installations under computer room floors, draining gutters, electrical rooms, storage rooms etc.

The software is very user friendly and allows him to control and monitor all the control systems in the building.
