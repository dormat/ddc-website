UniArt Software

An interface that allows the connection of tens of thousands of control points to an unlimited amount of computers (electricity, air conditioning, process, plumbing, chief engineer, etc.) UniArt is the excellent solution in public and industrial buildings for controlling heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems (HVAC), energy systems, lighting, fire detection (all companies with open protocols) and more.

UniWeb Software

UniWeb is the optimal solution in public and industrial buildings for controlling heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems (HVAC), energy systems, lighting, fire detection (all companies with open protocols) and more.

DigiPoint Controller

DigiPoint is an independent controller that includes an internal clock that can be programmed using a ladder diagram and software routines developed for different uses, the controller was specially designed for controlling and monitoring lighting systems and electromechanical systems in public and industrial buildings.

The controller is specially adapted for hotels, office buildings, hospitals, universities, museums, clean industry and more.

VeroPoint Controller

VeroPoint is the optimal solution for system control and monitoring In public buildings, industry, hospitals, hotels.

SuperBrain Controller

SuperBrain is an optimal controller for computerized control of air conditioning, heating, ventilation and plumbing systems in public buildings.

SuperBrain offers a variety of specially designed advanced software for electromechanical systems in hotels, hospitals, universities, public and industrial buildings.

SuperBrain DR Controller

SuperBrain is an optimal controller for computerized control of air conditioning, heating, ventilation and plumbing systems in public buildings.

SuperBrain offers a variety of specially designed advanced software For electro-mechanical systems in hotels, hospitals, universities, public and industrial buildings.

ElNet XP Controller

An automatic switching controller is used to operate drainage and sewage pumps and is found in almost every public building today, the controller is used to automatically control the level of rainwater or sewage, including automatic switching between two pumps in each operation in order to balance the working hours and the introduction of an off-duty pump for reinforcement when necessary.

We find these systems in factories, hotels, hospitals, universities, office buildings and any other public building where a reliable system is required to operate pumps for draining rainwater or sewage.
