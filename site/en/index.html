    Our highlight products

    All products
    ElNet PQ/GR Meter

    ElNet PQ is certified by a testing laboratory to the exclusive Class A standard for measuring the quality of electricity.

    ElNet LT Meter

    Has a measurement accuracy of 0.2% (0.1% by special order).

    ElNet MC-8 Meter

    Three-phase energy measurements in Touz

    ElNet LTE Meter

    Measurement of voltage, current, energy, frequency, power and power multiplier.

    ElNet CO Transfer Switch

    The ElNet CO system is designed for an efficient exchange between two power supply sources (electricity company / generator) in the event of a malfunction in one of the power supply systems.

    ElNet PFC Controller

    Support for up to 6 levels.

    ElNet XP Controller

    An automatic switching controller is used to operate drainage and sewage pumps and is found in almost every public building today, the controller is used to automatically control the level of rainwater or sewage, including automatic switching between two pumps in each operation in order to balance the working hours and the introduction of an off-duty pump for reinforcement when necessary.
